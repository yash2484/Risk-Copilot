# Credit Line Increase Guidelines

**Document ID:** RISK-POL-002 | **Version:** 1.8 | **Owner:** Credit Risk Management | **Last Reviewed:** Q1 2025

## Section 1: Purpose and Scope

This document defines the eligibility criteria, approval thresholds, and exception process for customer-initiated and proactive credit line increases (CLI). It applies to all consumer card products. Small business products follow a separate policy (RISK-POL-002B).

## Section 2: Baseline Eligibility Criteria

A customer must satisfy ALL of the following baseline criteria to be considered for any credit line increase:

- Account age of at least 6 months for existing customers (18 months for New_To_Credit segment)
- No active delinquency at the time of evaluation
- No charge-off events in the trailing 24 months
- Utilization ratio between 0.40 and 0.85 in the trailing 90 days (consistently low utilization is interpreted as low product engagement)
- No more than one cross-border Level 2 escalation in the trailing 12 months

## Section 3: Approval Tiers by Risk Score

Maximum approved increase amounts depend on the customer's combined risk score at the time of evaluation:

### 3.1 Risk Score 0–25 (Low Risk)
- Maximum increase: 50% of current credit limit, capped at $15,000
- Approval channel: Auto-approval, no analyst review
- Review cadence: Eligible every 6 months

### 3.2 Risk Score 26–35 (Moderate-Low Risk)
- Maximum increase: 30% of current credit limit, capped at $10,000
- Approval channel: Auto-approval if income verification is current; otherwise analyst review
- Review cadence: Eligible every 9 months

### 3.3 Risk Score 36–44 (Moderate Risk)
- Maximum increase: 15% of current credit limit, capped at $3,000
- Approval channel: Mandatory analyst review with income re-verification
- Review cadence: Eligible every 12 months

### 3.4 Risk Score 45 and Above (Elevated Risk)
- Not eligible for credit line increase regardless of utilization or tenure
- Customer-initiated requests must be declined with reason code CLI-INELIG-RISK
- Re-evaluation possible after 6 months of risk score below 45

## Section 4: Segment-Specific Rules

- **New_To_Credit:** Minimum 18 months of account history required regardless of risk score. First CLI capped at $1,500.
- **Subprime:** Not eligible for proactive CLI offers. Customer-initiated requests reviewed manually with stricter income verification.
- **Premium and High_Value:** Eligible for accelerated review (3-month minimum between requests) if risk score is below 25.

## Section 5: Documentation Requirements

All approved CLIs must include: prior risk score, current utilization ratio, months since last delinquency, monthly income at time of decision, and the specific approval tier applied. Records are retained for 7 years per regulatory requirement.

## Section 6: Exceptions and Overrides

Exceptions to approval tiers require written justification from a Senior Credit Risk Analyst and are limited to 5% of monthly approvals per analyst. All exceptions are reviewed in the monthly credit risk committee.
